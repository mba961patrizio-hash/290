Privacy Policy

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how USOX handles user privacy regarding the use of the application. We are committed to protecting your privacy and security.

Data Collection

USOX does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information.

The application operates entirely on the device and does not require an account, login, or backend connectivity for its core functionality. No user data is uploaded to any third-party servers.

Local Data Usage

Any data generated during gameplay or app usage is stored locally on your device. You can delete all local data by uninstalling the application or clearing app cache/storage in your device settings.

Permissions

The application may request standard Android permissions necessary for its operation, such as:

  • Storage: To save high scores or settings locally.
  • Network: (Only if used for updates or ads, though this specific app design focuses on offline capability). For this specific application build, network access is minimized to zero for privacy protection.

No sensitive permissions like Contacts, SMS, or precise Location are requested.

Third-Party Services

This application does not integrate third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. No data is shared with external partners.

Children's Privacy

This application is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children.

Changes to Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy, you may contact the developer of USOX through the app's official support channels.
